Built for watching with people, not screens. Paper cards, real conversation, and a room that goes quiet when the ball is in the box.

06 · FAQ

Questions

Do I pay to watch?
No. You make a small reservation that counts toward your food and drink.
Where is it?
We're a pop-up for now. Match nights take place at a partner venue in central Madrid, announced to members first. A place of our own comes later.
When?
During the Champions League knockout phase, one match a week to start.
Can I bring a group?
Yes. Teams and friend groups can reserve tables together.
What language is the commentary?
The standard Spanish broadcast. The crowd is international and everyone is welcome.
