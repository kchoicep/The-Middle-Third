The Middle Third

Concept Summary

The Middle Third is a new kind of third place for football fans in Madrid: not a bar and not your living room, but a cinemma-style space whee you watch the biggest matches on the big screen with reserved seats, premium sound, and a respectful, mixed-fan community. It exists for people who love the atmosphere of the stadium, but want the comfort, focus, and safety of a curated community experience.

Join the early community by leaving your email to become a founding member, help shape the first events, and get priority access to Madrid's first football cinemma experience.
